What changed from direction B

· Solid borders everywhere. Dashed lines no longer appear anywhere, so nothing reads as a drop zone.

· The entry screen explains the three steps instead of showing an empty outline.

· Adding books uses the same label and field as the entry screen. Results show the ISBN, a cover slot and the first shop that has the book, and the basket sits at the bottom.

· The result has three tabs: 1 parcel · 2 parcels, cheapest · Custom, choose stores.

· Custom: switch shops off, and their books move to the others, the cheapest way.

· Every book opens a detail screen with a button to that shop’s listing, so you can fill each cart yourself.

System

Bricolage Grotesque for display, Inter for text. White ground, kraft #f4efe6 for parcels only, green #14532d for the chosen answer, ink #1a1a1a for known values. Unknowns are a solid orange tag reading “not printed”. Shops we left out sit on a grey panel. Radii: 24 for parcels, 16 for fields, pills fully rounded. Every control is at least 44px.

About the numbers

Real launch data: 515,16 · 558,73 (+43,57) · 530,45 (+15,29) · Amazon.de free from 262,19 · Bookzone 10,90 + 1,99 under 200 · eMAG excluded. The per-book prices, the € rate, Libris’s delivery terms and the edge-case baskets are made up, built to add up exactly to those totals. ISBNs are shown as placeholders.
